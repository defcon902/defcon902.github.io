# DC902 Code of Conduct

DC902 is committed to building a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful community where people can share knowledge, explore technical topics, and support each other.

We are hackers. Curiosity is encouraged.  
Malice is not.  
Know the difference.

This Code of Conduct applies to all DC902 spaces, including Discord, online platforms, and in-person events.

---

## Expected Behavior

Members are expected to:

- Be respectful and considerate in discussions.
- Communicate thoughtfully and avoid personal attacks.
- Respect differing opinions, backgrounds, and experience levels.
- Foster constructive dialogue around technical topics.
- Comply with moderator guidance when asked.

---

## Harassment & Disruptive Conduct

Harassment or discrimination of any kind will not be tolerated.

This includes, but is not limited to:

- Targeted harassment, bullying, or intimidation  
- Hate speech or discriminatory language  
- Doxxing or sharing private information without consent  
- Threats of violence  
- Repeated unwanted contact  
- Disruptive behavior that interferes with community participation  

If someone asks you to stop a behavior, stop.

---

## Responsible Conduct & Legal Boundaries

DC902 is a community for learning, research, and technical exploration. Discussion of security vulnerabilities, reverse engineering, offensive techniques, and related topics is welcome when framed responsibly and ethically.

However, DC902 platforms and events may not be used to:

- Coordinate, solicit, or promote illegal activity.
- Provide actionable guidance intended to harm individuals, organizations, or infrastructure.
- Solicit assistance for ongoing unlawful activity.
- Buy, sell, trade, or distribute illegal goods or services.
- Encourage unlawful behavior in community spaces.

Discussions should focus on **education, defense, ethics, and research** — not real-world harm.

Moderators may intervene or remove content that crosses these boundaries.

---

## Enforcement & Moderation

DC902 uses a one-strike warning policy:

- **First violation** → Official warning  
- **Second violation** → Ban from DC902 spaces  

Warnings are documented by moderators.

### Immediate Ban

In extreme situations — including but not limited to credible threats, doxxing, explicit harassment, hate speech, malicious activity, or clear encouragement of harmful illegal conduct — moderators may, by consensus, issue an immediate ban without prior warning.

Moderator decisions are final.

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness a violation of this Code of Conduct, contact a moderator or organizer directly via private message.

Reports will be handled as confidentially and fairly as possible.
